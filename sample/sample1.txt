          We would like to share with you on the need to seek HDB's prior approval should you have plans to rent out your flat or bedrooms.

 

2        We wish to share that only Singapore Citizen flat owners may rent out their whole flat with prior approval from HDB after fulfilling the requisite Minimum Occupation Period (MOP). You can rent out your spare bedrooms with HDB’s prior approval, if you own a 3-room or bigger flat, regardless of the MOP or citizenship of the flat owners. However, if you own a 1 or 2-room flat, you are not allowed to rent out your bedroom. You must continue to stay in the flat while the bedroom is rented out. All occupants must also be registered with HDB.

 

3        You may disregard the advisory if you are not considering to rent out your flat/bedroom or have already obtained approval to do so by the time you received this. Should you require further information, you may refer to our website at www.hdb.gov.sg or write to me at Kenny_CJ_ANG@hdb.gov.sg.
